# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all participants in this project and its community to uphold this code of conduct through their actions.

## Our Standards

### ✅ Behavior That Contributes to Our Community

- **Be respectful** — Respect differing opinions, experiences, and perspectives
- **Be inclusive** — Welcome people of all backgrounds and identities
- **Be constructive** — Provide helpful feedback and focus on the idea, not the person
- **Be patient** — Understand that people have different levels of experience
- **Communicate clearly** — Be clear and kind in all interactions
- **Support others** — Help each other grow and learn

### ❌ Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment** — Unwelcome conduct based on protected characteristics
- **Discrimination** — Treating people unfairly based on identity
- **Abuse** — Insults, name-calling, threats, or personal attacks
- **Spam** — Repetitive, off-topic, or commercial messages
- **Exclusion** — Deliberately isolating or excluding community members
- **Bad faith** — Acting without genuine intent to help or participate

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Using derogatory or hateful language
- Sexual harassment or unwanted advances
- Publishing private information without consent
- Trolling, flame wars, or intentional disruption
- Advocating for harm or violence
- Impersonation or deception

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (GitHub, Discord, discussions, email)
- Project contributions (code, issues, PRs, comments)
- Representation of the project in public spaces
- Community events (online meetings, conferences, etc.)

## Enforcement

### How to Report

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code:

1. **Don't engage** — Don't respond to trolls or provocative behavior
2. **Document it** — Save evidence (screenshots, links, dates)
3. **Report privately** — Email [coc@jsebot.dev](mailto:coc@jsebot.dev)

**In your report, include:**

- Description of the incident
- Who was involved (usernames, not real names unless they shared them)
- When it happened
- Any evidence (messages, links)
- What impact it had
- What you'd like to see happen

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment** — We'll respond within 48 hours
2. **Investigation** — We'll gather facts and context
3. **Decision** — We'll determine appropriate action
4. **Resolution** — We'll communicate the outcome
5. **Follow-up** — We'll check in to ensure behavior changed

### Possible Actions

Depending on severity and context:

- **Gentle reminder** — First-time minor violations
- **Warning** — Continued problematic behavior
- **Temporary mute** — Short-term silence on channels (24-72 hours)
- **Temporary ban** — Temporary removal from community (1-4 weeks)
- **Permanent ban** — Removal from the community

## Community Guidelines

### GitHub Issues & PRs

- **Stay on topic** — Keep discussions relevant to the issue
- **Search first** — Check if your issue/PR already exists
- **Be clear** — Provide context and reproducible examples
- **Respect decisions** — Accept that not all ideas will be implemented
- **No spam** — Don't promote unrelated products or services

### Discord & Discussions

- **Be welcoming** — Say hi! This is a friendly place
- **Use threads** — Keep conversations organized
- **Search before asking** — Your question might be answered already
- **No self-promotion** — Unless explicitly invited
- **Respect quiet hours** — No @everyone pings for non-urgent items

### Code Reviews

- **Assume good intent** — Assume the person is trying their best
- **Focus on the code, not the person** — Comment on the idea, not the developer
- **Be specific** — Explain _why_ something is an issue, not just that it is
- **Acknowledge effort** — Thank people for their contributions
- **Learn together** — Code review is an opportunity to grow

### Discussions & Debates

- **Listen actively** — Try to understand other perspectives
- **Disagree respectfully** — You can disagree without being disagreeable
- **Stay logical** — Base arguments on evidence, not emotion
- **Agree to disagree** — You don't need to convince everyone
- **Move on** — Don't rehash settled debates

## Inclusion & Diversity

We recognize and value the diversity of our community. JSEBot is for everyone:

- **Different backgrounds** — Any ethnicity, religion, culture, nationality
- **Different abilities** — Including disabilities and neurodivergence
- **Different experiences** — Beginners to experts, all welcome
- **Different perspectives** — We learn from disagreement
- **Different identities** — Any gender identity, sexual orientation, age, etc.

**We commit to:**

- Using inclusive language (they/them if unsure of pronouns)
- Providing accommodations (captions, alt text, accessible formats)
- Amplifying underrepresented voices
- Investigating bias in code and processes
- Being willing to be corrected

## Mental Health & Well-Being

Open source should be fun. Here are some healthy boundaries:

- **Take breaks** — You don't need to fix every issue
- **Avoid burnout** — Contributing is voluntary
- **Respect others' time** — Not everyone can respond immediately
- **Ask for help** — It's okay to say you're overwhelmed
- **Celebrate wins** — Acknowledge progress and effort

If you're struggling with the pace or stress of contributing, that's okay. Please reach out.
